Sophisticated Physics Modules

☀️

Radiation

Two-stream shortwave and longwave radiation with gas absorption (CO₂, H₂O, O₃) and cloud interactions

🌧️

Convection

Mass-flux scheme with CAPE-based triggering for deep and shallow convection with momentum transport

☁️

Cloud Microphysics

Two-moment scheme with liquid and ice phases, autoconversion, accretion, and sedimentation

💨

Boundary Layer

TKE-based turbulence with Monin-Obukhov similarity theory for surface fluxes and mixing

🌱

Land Surface

Multi-layer soil model with heat diffusion, moisture transport, snow, and evapotranspiration

🌊

Ocean

Mixed layer model with ocean heat transport and sea ice thermodynamics with albedo feedback

Technical Capabilities

Atmospheric Dynamics

  • Primitive equations on rotating sphere
  • Spherical geometry with metric terms
  • Sigma vertical coordinates
  • 3rd-order Runge-Kutta integration
  • Energy conservation diagnostics
  • Polar filtering for stability

Numerical Methods

  • Multiple time-stepping schemes
  • Centered finite differences
  • Spectral energy analysis
  • CFL stability checking
  • Physical constraint enforcement
  • Horizontal diffusion

Configuration Options

  • Resolution: 32-96 longitude points
  • 16-48 latitude points
  • 10-32 vertical levels
  • CO₂: 200-1200 ppmv
  • Duration: 1-100 days
  • Multiple atmospheric profiles
